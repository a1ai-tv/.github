<h1 align="center">A1/AI</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Furniture with tradition since 1994.</strong></p>

A1/AI is a mockumentary web series about StolMarTex of Poznań: kitchens, wardrobes, made-to-measure fittings and furniture for hotels. Every order has its own sheet of paper, every problem its own phone call, and every topic starts in cell A1. The question is whether it has to end there.

## Inside the company

- About 85 people and eleven systems, one of them a squared notebook.
- `MASTER_FINAL_v7_OSTATECZNY.xlsx`, "The Beast" printer and a fax that still gets used.
- One meeting room, called A1, where every topic begins.
